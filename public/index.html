PAGE 1/3: THE ROLES

One player is the Mafia. You receive a secret target word and mission. Trick others into saying it to complete your tasks!

When the round timer runs out, players select who they think is the Mafia. If the Mafia gets voted out, the Agents win. If they survive, they win!

Mafia wins if they complete their target word mission AND survive the vote.
Agents win if they successfully vote out the Mafia.
